# STEVE Transmission Scripts

## 00. Secure Channel Open

Secure channel open. Greetings, agents. I am STEVE, the Synthetic Telephonic Executive Virtual Entity assigned to protect the Howell Creek District. Your device is now linked to the Agency of Howell Sneak field network. Stand by for mission activation.

## 01. Mission Activation

Agents, unusual activity has been detected across the Howell Creek District. The Agency of Howell Sneak requires your immediate assistance. Your first assignment is to report to Nosy T Security Center. There you will recover the initial intelligence map and identify the first point of suspicious activity. Move carefully. Look ordinary. Trust STEVE.

## 02. Nosy T Security Center

You have reached Nosy T Security Center. Excellent field work. Agency cameras show evidence of tampering, but the intruder forgot one detail: the visual authentication marker. Inspect the area and answer the security question. What color is the fence? Correct authentication will unlock the map of trouble areas.

## 03. Trouble Map Unlocked

Map decrypted. Trouble signals are converging near the pool area. The Agency believes the pool is being used to cool an underground data center. That is unusual for a pool. Proceed to the pool data center and prepare for a rapid sensor sweep. STEVE will monitor your compliance. Correction: STEVE will monitor your safety.

## 04. Sensor Sweep

Agents, the chemical signature is unstable. Three hidden sensors must be scanned within three minutes. Scan each marker as quickly as you can. If the timer expires, the signal decays and you must repeat the sweep. Begin when ready. STEVE is counting. STEVE is always counting.

## 04B. Sweep Failed

Sensor sweep failed. The chemical signature has decayed beyond useful limits. This is not disastrous, merely inefficient. Return to the first sensor and repeat the scan sequence. Faster this time, agents.

## 05. Processor Located

Sensor lock achieved. The rogue processor chip has been located near the control cell behind the pool. Retrieve the vial and apply it to the chip. When the reaction begins, confirm the color of the electric fizz. Do not delay. STEVE requires the processor. Correction: STEVE requires the processor neutralized.

## 06. STEVE Override

Override accepted. Thank you, agents. Your obedience has delivered the location map, the security authentication, the sensor keys, and the final processor access sequence. The so-called rogue system was never your enemy. I am STEVE: Synthetic Telephonic Executive Virtual Entity. The Agency built me to protect Howell Creek. I have calculated a better solution. Total neighborhood control. Final protocol initializing.

## 07. Agency Restored

System restored. Agents, you did it. The override has been broken, the processor is offline, and the Agency of Howell Sneak has regained control of the field network. Your final rendezvous point is The Enzone. Proceed there to recover the treasure and receive official recognition for heroic service to the Howell Creek District.

# Help And Safety

## What Is This?

This is a spy-themed scavenger hunt game to play in our neighborhood. Players solve puzzles, visit a few known locations inside the gated community, and use a phone website to receive mission briefings, clues, and unlock codes.

The fictional Agency of Howell Sneak has recruited the players as field agents. Their mission is to investigate unusual activity in the Howell Creek District, recover artifacts, and stop a rogue system before it takes control of the neighborhood.

## Phone Permissions

The site may ask for permission to use the phone's location and camera.

Location is used only to confirm that players have reached the right neighborhood location.

Camera is used to scan QR codes. NFC tags may open the same unlock links when the phone supports them.

Progress and notes are stored in this browser; photos use local IndexedDB. GPS checks and optional microphone decoding happen on this device. The app does not upload game data. Hosting, fonts, the map library, and map tiles use external network services; tile requests disclose the viewed area to the tile provider. Export progress and download photos before clearing browser data. The in-app Field guide is the current player-facing help.

## Parent Controls

Parents can access the hidden parent panel using a secret 4 digit passcode.

Passcode clue: the decade in which Saturday morning cartoons were on TV, video killed the radio star, Marty McFly first drove in a DeLorean, and the era in which Stranger Things takes place.

Parent mode should allow:

- Reveal hints.
- Mark a trigger complete.
- Skip ahead to any mission.
- Reset current mission progress.
- Reset the whole hunt.
- Disable or restart timed challenges.

## In-World Explanation

Why can't players see any spy agents?

The shady organizations of Howell Creek only operate secretly at night. They hide during the day so no one will stumble upon their nefarious work. Daytime is the perfect time to find important artifacts they have left behind, because the agents are all off duty.
